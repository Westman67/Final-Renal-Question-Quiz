# R2 tonicity question revision

## Scope

Six scored questions from the three R2 body-fluid compartment diagrams were revised. The images, variants, source groups, question IDs, citations, and total bank size remain unchanged.

The former items asked learners to read compartment-width or graph-height directions directly. Each diagram now supports two integrated tasks.

1. Classify the complete post-infusion pattern as hypertonic, hypotonic, or isotonic volume expansion.
2. Connect the tonicity change to transcellular water movement, cell volume, and final osmolality.

Page 23 defines hypertonic, isotonic, and hypotonic solutions by solute concentration. Page 26 states the following relationships used in the revised questions.

- Hypertonic addition raises extracellular concentration and volume. Water shifts from cell interior to exterior, increasing intracellular concentration.
- Isotonic addition increases extracellular volume with no fluid shift and no concentration change.
- Hypotonic addition increases extracellular volume while lowering extracellular concentration. Water shifts from cell exterior to interior, increasing intracellular volume and lowering intracellular concentration.

No outside sources or unsupported clinical examples were added.
