# Renal picture quiz learner-flag remediation

Reviewed 2026-10-06. Scope was the five open learner flags and all 95 scored R1–R9 questions. The 62 manual originals were treated as immutable. No outside sources were used.

| Flag | Decision | Course basis |
|---|---|---|
| R6 proximal water routes | Rewrite to test the lower paracellular water route instead of asking for both printed routes. | R6 Proximal Tubule RLS p. 23 |
| R6 amino-acid and drug transport | Mask both lower substrate labels and the word secretion. Keep both directional pathways visible. | R6 Proximal Tubule RLS p. 26 |
| R6 apical Na⁺/H⁺ exchange | Ask for the hidden secreted ion and mask its actual H⁺ label, not just the exchanger name. | R6 Proximal Tubule RLS p. 22 |
| R5 low-pressure TGF vessel | Replace the direct starburst-label question with a RPF/Pgc prediction. Mask the afferent/efferent names and printed outcome boxes, while preserving the caliber arrows now needed for the new question. | R5 Autoregulation RLS p. 12; R4 Renal Plasma Flow RLS p. 16 |
| R7 TAL cotransporter | Test the missing middle ion and count; mask the printed 2 Cl⁻ and transporter name while preserving the Na⁺ and K⁺ streams. | R7 Countercurrent Mechanism RLS p. 12 |

The same answer-leak standard was applied to three sibling R5 TGF questions, the R6 bicarbonate apical-product question, both R6 transcellular/paracellular route variants, and both R9 glutamine/ammonia questions. In all, 12 quiz-local derivatives were revised and 13 scored questions had content or image changes. The R9 diagram changes are grounded in R9 Acid-Base Regulation RLS p. 22.

Contact-sheet review covered the remaining R1, R2, R4, and R8 scored variants; targeted diagram review covered R5, R6, R7, and R9. No further direct answer-text leakage requiring correction was found. Every modified derivative was then opened individually at native size. The 95-question count, source groups, and original image hashes are unchanged.

The local app and static `dist` folder were rebuilt. This audit does not establish that the separately hosted website has been redeployed.
